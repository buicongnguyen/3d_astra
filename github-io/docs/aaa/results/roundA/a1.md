# A1 combat readability

Compared the integrated sprite and phone branches with the Round A release build using the same 15 shot fixtures at 390×844 and 1440×900. The before and after strips are in a1/. Heavy fireball billboards are 1.50× their previous dimensions at the same zoom. The tracer and its fading echoes span at least 0.25 seconds. Projectile flight times are unchanged. Building collapses use three staged blasts (previously six). Removed the render-time camera translation and roll on all devices. The Medic displays a cross and the Engineer a wrench. The draw paths reuse the two existing sprite batches and procedural atlas.

Independent review of 30 strips: tracer 6.5, strafe 8, shell 8, rocket 7.5, lob 7, flak 6.5, beam 7.5, rail 7.5, flamer 8, heal 7.5, missile 7, bomb 8, firebomb 8, walker 7.5, strike 8. Mean 7.50 vs the prior recorded 6.8; different reviewers. Tracers on sand and flak against trees remain the hardest to read. Brief occlusion limits further blast enlargement.

Unit suite: 1,090 passed, 28 skipped, zero failures. Production build passed. The strips exercise isolated weapons; dense-battle flash frequency and repair readability on a physical handset remain unmeasured. Visual combat, air and feel passed at 20× CPU slowdown. Readability, audio feedback and all 34 render-budget scenarios passed at 10× on SwiftShader.
