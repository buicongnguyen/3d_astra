# A6 accessible palettes and sizing

Three team palettes now override mission and skirmish team colours, glows, rings, minimap and HUD. Manual swatches and the Gold/Violet shortcut remain available. Settings persist 90%, 100%, 115% or 130%. Portrait and landscape phone battles retain their audited dock footprint. Menus use the chosen scale. The Noto Sans KR subset was rebuilt from the upstream variable font.

All eight real control combinations (four per viewport) applied and their Settings action footer fitted the viewport. See the paired battle and Settings screenshots. Tests covered all three presets, four distinct teams, persistence, invalid values, older saves and catalog glyph coverage.
