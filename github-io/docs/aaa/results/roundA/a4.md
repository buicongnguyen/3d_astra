# A4 world edge

Two low ridgelines form a continuous outland beyond the map lip, with biome colours on daylight maps and the same transition to the mission sky in night and storm. Geometry is baked into the existing vertex-coloured backdrop. Existing directional sun, hemisphere fill, contact shadow and pooled effect-core glow are retained. No post-processing pass was introduced.

Release screenshots cover every available skirmish scenario and both campaign missions. Timings below are median JavaScript submission time over 12 draws on the local emulated phone, not full present times or physical phone measurements.
