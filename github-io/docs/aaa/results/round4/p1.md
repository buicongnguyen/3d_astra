tames on Relaxed, and on Normal its late storms and parties do not cost the bot a game); "uses" stays within one win
(1v2 Normal with capture points 3 → 2) or gets easier. Fast and Relentless are harder with the rule on, as chosen.

**How the gate was reached (two fixes, both kept).** The first bench failed 1v2 Normal (3 → 0) for reasons that had
nothing to do with storms or Wyrms: (1) the three guards took ids from the shared counter, and shifting every later id
by 3 alone reshuffles knife-edge matches (a control run with only `nextId += 3` gave exactly the rule's results); the
guards now take ids from their own range. (2) Replacing the crossroads ring with a new Spire ring changed every rival's
opening ring crews; the crossroads ring itself is now the Spire. With both, a Wild match is byte-identical to the
rule-off match until a power or creature is used.
