The audit used authored damage and active shooter stats. Ground splash applies to Tank, Walker, Breaker, Scorcher, Hawk, Balloon and Gunner. The basic Tower, Rampart, anti-tank infantry and Glider deal direct damage despite cannon, rocket or bomb visuals. Ranger, Worker, Vanguard, Scrapper, Rider, Drone, Shade, Watcher, Stalker and the three boss gun definitions also use direct contact. The previous searing death treatment could imply burning from direct energy weapons. Active Defence crew upgrades can grant splash, and ground splash is suppressed against flyers.

The running cap is approximately 24 FX records per ten visible troops at full quality, with a floor of 12. The governor removes small, distant detail first, coalesces protected hits by selected target and allows physical projectiles to complete their paths. Bright highlights share a real-time 340 ms gate. Reduced motion suppresses those highlights and moving Ranger streaks. All work is presentational and adds no camera shake.

The final unit run passed 1,105 tests, with 28 existing skips. New checks exercise the damage matrix, ordered culling, selected-target reservations, the flash gate, direct rocket contacts and buffer reuse. The production build succeeded.
