# F6 · short, grounded weapon impacts

The Tank's shell leaves a small hot core on the armour at contact. By the 490 ms sample after firing, the tank's shape and its nearby infantry are visible. The anti-tank rocket makes a single-target spark. A collapsing Barracks briefly lights its roof and then exposes the surrounding soldiers.

| Cue | Rendering |
| --- | --- |
| Rifle in flight | One quarter of the original live width, length and glow. The Ranger uses one moving atlas streak, with a procedural fallback. Its position still traverses the full shot distance. |
| Muzzle and contact | A 50 ms muzzle core and a 45 ms direct-hit glint. A real-time 340 ms gate controls bright highlights across the fight. |
| Ground splash | Two ring-free atlas variants, selected with the cosmetic generator. Size varies by up to 20% within the two-target-width cap. Rotation and spark directions vary. The core brightens for 50 ms and cools to translucent grey-brown by 160 ms. |
| Unloaded sprites | A short procedural core, a normal-blended orange-to-brown body and normal-blended smoke with slight wind. |
| Impact dust | The dust samples the actual ground under the impact and starts 0.06 m above its surface. The palette follows sand, grass, stone and bridge material, with a pale fallback for snow. Size varies around the quarter-scale live size. |
| Scorch and rings | Ground splash can leave a small scorch. Direct unit hits leave sparks and dust. Artillery retains its advance landing warning. |
