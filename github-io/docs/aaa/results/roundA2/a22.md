# A2.2 Team paint at phone scale

The boards use all 37 shipping unit and building GLBs, including the three bosses. Each model occupies a 24 × 24 pixel tile, viewed from the game camera angle over sampled Riverlands grass or Dunes sand. Read each pair left to right as ally and foe. The four pairs use Default, Protanopia, Deuteranopia and Tritanopia.

All 592 comparisons changed visibly at the native pixel size (at least 12 levels in one RGB channel). This measures the presence of the paint, not perceptual recognition. The team stripe follows the armour surface and articulation. Shells already carrying team paint receive a darker team stripe. Boss vertex paint updates when the player changes palette during a match. The offline wing uses a material stripe.

The roster, boss, batching and model tests passed (23). Release model-loading, loading UI and offline-aircraft checks passed. Seven representative strict render-budget scenarios passed in the WebGL phone preview. The mixed-faction battle used 157 draws before and after and 306,183 → 309,052 triangles (limit 315,000). The 300-unit stress case used 99 draws and 1,234,030 triangles. The boards were generated in Chromium with SwiftShader. Human recognition and physical-device timing remain unmeasured.
