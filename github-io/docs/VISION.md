# Vision

Frontier Command is an original single-player real-time strategy game for the browser. It must be good on a phone and on a PC, and it must stay smooth on both.

**The game.** You lead the Meridian expedition across a frontier of seven maps: build a small economy, raise an army, and win twelve story missions in three acts, or play free skirmishes against a computer opponent at four speeds. Fights are decided by positioning, abilities, stances and veterancy, not by clicking faster.

**The bar.** A polished console-game feel: vivid warm colour, readable silhouettes, living worlds, a story with reasons behind every mission, clear and comfortable interface at every screen size.

**Principles**
- Phone and PC are both first-class. Every screen is checked at 1440x900, 390x844 and 844x390; text is never under 11 px and touch targets are at least 44 px.
- Performance is a feature. Draw calls, triangles and frame time are measured before and after every visual change.
- The rules are deterministic and testable without a browser. The same options and command log give the same match.
- Everything is original: code, art, music and story. Nothing is licensed or borrowed.
- Honest tests: a green run must mean the game works.

**Not goals now.** Online multiplayer (planned after the rebuild; the command layer keeps it possible), porting new features to the Godot twin.
