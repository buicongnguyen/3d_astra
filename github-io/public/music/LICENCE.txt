Frontier Command game music (final/*.m4a)
=========================================

What these files are
--------------------
Five original instrumental cues for the browser RTS "Frontier Command": title
(loop), campaign (loop), defence (loop), victory (sting), defeat (sting).
Generated locally on 2026-11-13 on the owner's RTX 4080 SUPER with ACE-Step 1.5
(turbo DiT "acestep-v15-turbo" + 5 Hz LM "acestep-5Hz-lm-1.7B", code commit
ca1e85fe9430179831e6bc6be790c332190a3866 of https://github.com/ACE-Step/ACE-Step-1.5),
text-to-music only, chain-of-thought rewriting off (captions used as written).
Then cut to whole bars (loops) or to the final 5-8 s phrase (stings),
loop-crossfaded, loudness-mastered (-16 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP
on the decoded AAC) and encoded to AAC 64 kbps by this batch's own scripts
(experiments/music-frontier-1113/tools). Loop points are in manifest.json.
No vocals (instrumental mode). No prompt names an artist, a song, an album or
a real person; no reference audio was used; nothing was sampled or downloaded
apart from the model weights.
