Frontier Command voice packs (public/voices/*.mp3)
==================================================

What these files are
--------------------
Spoken lines for the browser RTS "Frontier Command" (W3, 2026-11-25): one MP3 sprite per pack.
  meridian, ashfall, sunward, wardens, veil   unit replies (select / move / attack) and announcer
                                             lines of the player's faction
  story-<mission>[-2]                        the story lines of one campaign or Chronicle mission
                                             (story-m01 ... story-m12, story-ca1 ... story-cv4; a long
                                             mission has a second part, -2)
The faction lines are in src/voice-lines.json, the story lines in src/voice-story.json;
src/voice-packs.json holds the clip offsets.

How they were made
------------------
Synthesised locally on the owner's GPU with Kokoro-82M (hexgrad, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M),
stock voice packs only (am_michael, af_bella, bm_george, bm_lewis, am_fenrir, af_heart, am_echo,
bf_alice, af_nicole, bf_isabella, af_sarah, am_puck, bm_fable, af_aoede, bf_emma, am_onyx, af_kore,
af_nova). No cloud service, no cloned or
imitated person, no reference recording. Then processed per faction (radio band, drive, crackle,
ring modulation, chorus, room) and encoded to MP3 (mono, 24 kHz, 32 kbps) by
tools/voices/make-voices.py.

for the studio on 2026-10-21, voice_sync/LICENCE_NOTES.md). The generated audio is ours to ship.

Kokoro has no Vietnamese or Korean voices: Korean players, and every story line in Vietnamese,
use these English lines with the on-screen text in their language (Vietnamese unit and announcer lines: below).

generated locally with VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo (pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo, https://huggingface.co/pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo,
