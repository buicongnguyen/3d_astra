Frontier Command voice packs (public/voices/*.mp3)
==================================================

What these files are
--------------------
Spoken lines for the browser RTS "Frontier Command" (W3, 2026-11-25): one MP3 sprite per pack.
  meridian, ashfall, sunward, wardens, veil   unit replies (select / move / attack) and announcer
                                             lines of the player's faction
  story-m01                                  ten story lines of campaign mission 1 (Vale, Okoro, Hale)
The text of every line is in src/voice-lines.json; src/voice-packs.json holds the clip offsets.

How they were made
------------------
Synthesised locally on the owner's GPU with Kokoro-82M (hexgrad, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M),
stock voice packs only (am_michael, af_bella, bm_george, bm_lewis, am_fenrir, af_heart, am_echo,
bf_alice, af_nicole, bf_isabella, af_sarah, am_puck, bm_fable). No cloud service, no cloned or
imitated person, no reference recording. Then processed per faction (radio band, drive, crackle,
ring modulation, chorus, room) and encoded to MP3 (mono, 24 kHz, 32 kbps) by
tools/voices/make-voices.py.

Licence basis
-------------
Kokoro-82M model card: licence tag "apache-2.0"; the page calls the weights "Apache-licensed" (checked
for the studio on 2026-10-21, voice_sync/LICENCE_NOTES.md). The generated audio is ours to ship.
The engine and its phonemiser (misaki, espeak-ng fallback) are not shipped; only these MP3s are.
English only: Kokoro has no Vietnamese or Korean voices. Vietnamese and Korean players hear the
English lines with the on-screen text in their language.
