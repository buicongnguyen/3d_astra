Frontier Command: faction music candidates

Each faction gets a battle theme (it loops while you play that faction) and a short stinger (it plays when you pick the faction, when a match starts and when you win). All takes are original instrumentals made locally with ACE-Step 1.5 and mastered the same way as the game files (-16 LUFS, 64 kbps AAC). Labels are blind; one theme and one stinger per faction are already in the game build on branch claude/music, and you can swap any of them for another label here.

"Play as game loop" loops the file at its loop points exactly as the game does. "Hear the seam" starts 5 s before the loop point. Listen for: does it sound like that faction, a click or jump at the wrap, harsh highs, and whether it stays out of the way of unit voices.
